# Placeholder covers

The 30 covers shown for music without art (CHANGE_REQUESTS.md CR-08), in `core/ui/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.
`PlaceholderCovers` picks one from the track's folder, so an album keeps the same cover everywhere.

Made on 2026-10-23 with the owner's local ComfyUI and its `image_z_image_turbo.json` workflow (Z-Image Turbo bf16,
Qwen3-4B text encoder, `ae` VAE; 1024 × 1024, 8 steps, CFG 1, res_multistep / simple, AuraFlow shift 3), then resized
to 512 × 512 WebP (quality 80). 36 were generated; 6 were left out (three came out framed on a white wall, three were
too plain). Seed = 1000 + 7919 × (generated number).

Every prompt ends with: *full-bleed artwork that fills the whole image edge to edge, no border, no frame, no mockup, no wall, clean composition, rich detail, no text, no letters, no words, no typography, no logo*
